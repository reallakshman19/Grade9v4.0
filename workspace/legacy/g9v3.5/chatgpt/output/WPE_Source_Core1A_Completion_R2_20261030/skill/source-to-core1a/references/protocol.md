# Evidence-to-construction protocol — R1 candidate

## Purpose and two entry routes

**Book/syllabus route:** saved sources → source units and claim ledger → scope/canonical mapping → construction requirements → Core1A preparation dossier.

**Exam route:** SL1 custody/fitness → SL2 cognitive proposal → independent QRT decision → exact task requirements → the same preparation dossier.

The routes meet at a bound intellectual requirement, not at a shared topic name. Book concepts may be prepared before a reviewed exam link exists. They cannot thereby acquire an exam band, qualify coverage or prove teaching sufficiency. A source APPLY task may be a prerequisite bridge for JUSTIFY teaching without becoming JUSTIFY examination evidence.

No table row transfers authority to this skill. A book, publisher explanation, source URL, checksum or status enum alone qualifies none of the others.

## What a handoff must answer

- What exact source says what? Preserve edition/locator, raw claim, context, figures, source authority and contradictory evidence.
- What prior capabilities and conventions does this construction need? Separate particle/translational work, chosen body/system, point of application, frame and force conditions where relevant.
- What must the learner learn to construct? Name input/output roles, the obstacle and decisive bridge. Link required roles with dependencies; record a task's competing interpretation when applicable.
- Why may each consequential step be made? Bind actual candidate/qualified canonical references and source passages separately. Name a missing warrant as a gap.
- Which representation correspondences must be taught? Preserve object, symbol, sign, scale, axis and scope; identify whether an asset is printed, redrawn, authored or unavailable.
- What distinct non-exam example must the future teacher complete? A source worked example is an author reference. Reusing a protected Core2/Core1B answer as teacher content is not an acceptable shortcut.
- What boundary, wrong route, repair and independent check are justified? State hypotheses as hypotheses; do not fabricate learner observations.
- How will the future Core1B requirement correspond? Record the required operation and protected role now. Evidence that fresh Core1B actually exercises it must wait for the authored task.

## Native authoring boundary

Map to existing `package.schema.json` fields: microtopic entry assumptions, inferential jump, teaching path; each step's action/why_valid/inputs/output; relation conditions/checks; representation correspondence/scenes; construction-unit step, relation, representation and worked-anchor refs; exit task. These are **future authoring targets**, not fabricated native records.

The current `source-interpretation/v1` schema requires a genuine acquisition reference. This candidate does not invent one to force local excerpts through it. Its exact units can later be mapped after an acquisition exists. Similarly, `authoring-proposal/v1` needs a real request/packet and proposed native records; do not forge those digests or output an empty approved-looking package.

The current renderer derives Core1A↔Core2 navigation using canonical primary/secondary capability refs. Keep the semantic witness as an audit view; do not install reciprocal academic link lists as a competing authority. A capability join proves navigation correspondence, not intellectual equivalence.

## Teaching-to-reconstruction obligations

Carry B1 correct task, B2 signature, B3 complete teaching evidence, B4 fresh reconstruction, B5 protection/state, B6 diagnosis/transfer and B7 Physics/provenance. At preparation time B3/B4/B5/B6 are prospective obligations, not PASS claims. Later compare in both directions: every essential learner role sufficiently taught; every teaching role claimed as assessed actually elicited or honestly labelled support.

Do not force one teaching algorithm on seven operations. Classification needs defining criteria/exclusions; explanation needs warranted links; application needs applicability and execution; modelling needs selected assumptions; representation needs invariant-preserving correspondence; synthesis needs dependent bridges; justification needs claims/warrants/scope.
