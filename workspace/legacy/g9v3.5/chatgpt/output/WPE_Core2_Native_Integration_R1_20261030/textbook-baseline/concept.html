# WPE source routes — revised concept note

Revision 2026-10-30, textbook Core2 R1. Supersedes the earlier rigid question-bank/non-bank split; preserves frozen historical reports and deliveries.

## Owner correction and decision

A textbook is both a source of explanations and a source of questions. Its supplied questions are legitimate Core2 intake with textbook provenance. They do not need authenticated examination identity to exist in Core2. The book's JEE labels remain source claims until the original examination question is matched. A missing official key blocks an official-key claim, not an honestly labelled textbook question.

BOOK / SYLLABUS / SCANS
  - explanatory passages → exact units, claims, conditions and candidate warrants → Core1A preparation
  - existing illustrations / exercises → exact question and solution custody → SL1 → provisional SL2 → QRT decision → Core2 reader
QUESTION BANK → the same question pipeline, with its own source role and authenticity evidence

Both routes converge on exact task-to-construction requirements. QRT resolves operation/demand and source role; a binder establishes that Core1A teaches the actual intellectual requirement. Shared capability IDs alone do not prove teaching sufficiency. No new Smart Labelling stage or duplicate Physics Blueprint is introduced.
