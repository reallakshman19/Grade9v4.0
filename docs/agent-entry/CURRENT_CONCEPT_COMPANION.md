## Why this document matters

The concept report explains how exact book passages become claims, conditional warrants, construction requirements and native authoring obligations. It also explains why labels, matching IDs, solved examples and mechanically complete forms do not prove that a construction was taught. Every new Oracle and Core1A preparation/output agent must read it before designing that handoff.

Two routes meet at reviewed requirements and the construction binder:

1. Question-bank questions **and questions in the textbook** → SL1 identity/custody → provisional organization under pinned subject rules → SL2 intellectual requirement → distinct QRT adjudication → native Core2.
2. Exposition, syllabus and figures → exact source units → claim/condition ledger → candidate warrants and construction obligations → reviewed task join when available → qualified binder → Core1A authoring under the actual Owner scope.

The Physics Blueprint and topic adapter are inputs before classification. Optional learner P1 evidence must be saved before help/answer exposure; missing P1 never blocks learning or static question analysis. SL2 does not own Physics qualification or the final lesson. No SL3 is introduced.

## Construction acceptance and ownership

The binder must supply actual passage/task evidence for B1 target, B2 construction signature, B3 teaching sufficiency, B4 fresh reconstruction, B5 attempt protection, B6 diagnosis/changed-condition transfer and B7 Physics/provenance. A forward potential→force explanation does not establish reverse force→potential teaching. A resolved capability reference provides navigation, not semantic equivalence.

Shared owns canonical role/presentation/release contracts; subject Blueprint owns cognitive rules; canonical library owns qualified subject laws and conditions; adapter binds topic-specific instances; QRT resolves operation/demand/source role; the renderer projects native records. Grade9QA reconciles evidence and cannot issue reviewer approval. Neither the concept report nor this companion appoints a reviewer, selects an approved adapter, installs a skill or grants authoring/release.

Physics/WPE is a pilot within a Physics/Mathematics/Chemistry product. No Physics demand/warrant/adapter is inherited by the other subjects. Their first topics and approved Blueprints remain open.
