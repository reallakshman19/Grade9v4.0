# Smart Labelling 2: cognitive requirement and observed evidence

SL1 identifies and vets the source. SL2 proposes the intellectual requirement and records actual observations when available. QRT resolves operation, intrinsic demand and source role before placement. The binder subsequently establishes qualified Physics and Core1A/Core1B correspondence; the compiler preserves the result. Do not introduce Smart Labelling 3 or duplicate the shared Blueprint.

Read [record-shape.md](references/record-shape.md) for the retained v1 overlay. Read [hardening-contract.md](references/hardening-contract.md) for source binding, the compact cognitive seed, trusted review, observation fields and optional downstream gates.

## Analyze the task before seeing prior scores

Use the exact pinned SL1 question and answer status. Construct a sufficient route; identify the decisive role or linked roles and why competing operation interpretations are weaker. Keep wrong routes as hypotheses until actual learner evidence supports diagnosis. Use representations, independent checks and governing conditions that fit this operation, rather than forcing every task into a proof chain.

Score all five intrinsic-demand factors independently before comparing builder/previous proposals. Keep each factor's evidence and differences even when totals match. Preserve existing operation IDs and rubric bands. Neither teaching hints, long prose nor the number of cases manufactures D3/D4. Do not force a question into a desired coverage cell.

Supply the compact typed seed: actual inputs/output, decisive route roles, conditions, dependency/authority requirements, boundary checks, competing operations and demand evidence. It is an upstream requirement, not a completed lesson or Bridge Witness. SL2 records unqualified authority needs; it does not certify Physics.

## Preserve optional learner evidence

QUESTION mode remains valid without P1 and contains no claimed observation. EVIDENCE mode requires the actual unique first saved P1 attempt for this exact task/version. Copy original reasoning as well as answer, confidence and exposure. Keep missing help UNKNOWN, explicit help ASSISTED, and unsupported save history open. Never replace her reasoning with a cleaner solution or relabel Core1B as P1.

Keep correctness, capability interpretation and failure diagnosis separate from raw observation; these need their own current independent review. Never claim mastery from a correct option or a passing checker. Protect learner identity and original attempts in Owner-local custody.

## Handoff and check

Keep v1 `demand_proposal.status` PROPOSED. New records are UNREVIEWED; REVIEWED needs a distinct, trusted current receipt. Run `scripts/check_sl2.py` with the v2 contract and the relevant gate. `--binding` is only a legacy binding helper; `--legacy-only` grants no qualification.

QRT uses a separate saved first-scoring record and adjudication receipt. Resolve DIRECT_EXAM_EVIDENCE, PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE or AUTHORED_TRANSFER before one primary coverage home. A source APPLY bridge may support JUSTIFY teaching without becoming JUSTIFY exam evidence. Source question, bound task and family keep separate identities.

The binder owner supplies actual teaching/reconstruction spans, qualified warrants, current Blueprint/adapter pins and independent B1-B7 review. These are optional downstream inputs, not mandatory SL2 outputs. Missing academic, browser or learner acceptance stays open at the affected gate. Preserve all original teaching and assessment.
