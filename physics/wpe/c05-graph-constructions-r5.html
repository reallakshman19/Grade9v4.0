One graph operation does not fit every picture. A potential-position slope gives conservative force, force-position signed area gives work, velocity-time area gives displacement, and changes in kinetic energy give NET work—even when speed is plotted against position. Constant-power motion has its own dynamics, and not every force admits a scalar potential.
