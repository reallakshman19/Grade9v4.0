## The EX-M6 exception is substantive, not editorial

With the **hypothesized** bottom semicircle geometry and position \(φ_S=π/4\) measured from A along the arc (original figure not verified), the source's specified HORIZONTAL velocity component v is **not** total tangent speed. Its tangent speed at S is \(\sqrt2v\). Normal force generally changes horizontal velocity as P moves.

In the frictionless constrained model, let \(\lambda=v²/(gR)\), \(s=\sin φ\). Energy yields \(v_P(φ)²=2v²+2gR[\sin φ-1/\sqrt2]\), while \(t_Q=2R/v\).
Then
\[
\frac{t_P}{t_Q}=\frac{\sqrt{\lambda}}{2}\int_{\pi/4}^{\pi}\frac{dφ}{\sqrt{2\lambda+2(\sin φ-1/\sqrt2)}}.
\]
P reaches B only if \(\lambda\ge1/\sqrt2\). Direct numerical quadrature gives approximately **1.009** at \(\lambda=0.708\), but **0.864** at \(\lambda=1\): both opposite time orderings occur for admissible parameters under the hypothesized geometry. At the threshold, endpoint speed vanishes and the integral needs careful numerical handling. **Do not teach “P faster” or “P slower” as a universal law, nor substitute a contact-force lesson.** An authentic original figure and independent exam-source/key adjudication are required before any source-bound answer is certified.

## Ideal unstable-equilibrium clarification (scoped scientific correction)

The newly authored C6S **horizontal robot Task B** initially put the robot at exact rest opposite a fixed spring anchor. At that exact antipodal point the spring pulls purely radial and exerts **zero tangential force**. The robot does not spontaneously move from ideal exact rest; the computed nonzero speed is the **limit of trajectories initiated by a vanishing tangential displacement/perturbation**, with perturbation energy tending to zero. Its vertical boundary case uses the same limiting initial state.

The standard C6H **rest-at-crown exterior-dome example** has the same mathematical subtlety: gravitational tangent force is zero at the exact symmetric crown. The familiar detachment height `h=2R/3` is the **limit of slightly disturbed downhill starts** (e.g. release from a small positive crown angle), not spontaneous motion from perfect crown rest. The R4 Task A with positive initial downhill speed and Task B starting at 60° need no such qualification. Both clarifications are about authored ideal models and do not authenticate original source keys or figures. No academic admission is inferred.
