Demonstration Item: Conservative Energy Exchange in a Restoring Field

A particle of mass m=0.50kg moves along the x-axis under the action of an ideal conservative spring force F(x)=-kx, where k=50.0N/m. At position x=0.40m, the particle is released from rest (v0=0). No dissipative forces are present.

Physical Target Question: What is the kinetic energy K and speed v of the particle when it passes through the origin x=0?

Initial state (x0=0.40m,v0=0)⟶Final state (x1=0.00m,v1=?)

Rigorous Worked Derivation

1. Conservation of Mechanical Energy:

Einitial=Efinal⇒U(x0)+K(x0)=U(x1)+K(x1)

2. Evaluating at Initial State (x0=0.40m, v0=0):

U0=12kx02=12(50.0)(0.40)2=25×0.16=4.0J
K0=0J⇒Etot=4.0J

3. Evaluating at Origin (x1=0):

U(0)=12(50.0)(0)2=0J⇒K1=Etot-U(0)=4.0J

4. Solving for Instantaneous Velocity v1:

K1=12mv12⇒4.0=12(0.50)v12=0.25v12⇒v12=4.00.25=16.0⇒v1=4.0m/s

Worked-solution note (not an assessment of your explanation): The physical reasoning connects the zero-force point at the origin to maximum kinetic energy, rather than zero speed.
