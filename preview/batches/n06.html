Try: A 2 kg object moves from height 1 m to 4 m in approximately uniform gravitational field g=10 m/s². What is the gravitational potential change and work by gravity? If we add 50 J to the arbitrary potential reference C, what changes?

Hint 1For a consistent body–Earth system in uniform g, use U(y)=mgy+C.
Hint 2Compute UB−UA; a common constant C cancels. Gravity work is −ΔU.
Worked routeΔU=2×10×3=+60 J; Wgravity=−60 J. Adding 50 J to C increases both endpoint potentials by 50 J but leaves ΔU and Wgravity unchanged.
