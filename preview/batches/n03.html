The conceptual bridge

The particle work–energy theorem in an inertial frame is ΣWᵢ=ΔK. The sum must include all work on the same particle over the same interval. If you switch to an energy ledger with gravitational potential energy, gravity's work becomes −ΔU; never count it twice.

Fresh protected attempt

A 2 kg block initially travels at 2 m/s on a horizontal fixed surface. Over the next 3 m, a horizontal force of 12 N acts forward and kinetic friction of 4 N acts backward. Normal and gravity are perpendicular to displacement. Find final speed.

Hint 1 · RepresentSeparate applied, friction, normal and gravitational work on this block.
Hint 2 · ConnectCompute net work before using Kf−Ki. Initial kinetic energy is not zero.
