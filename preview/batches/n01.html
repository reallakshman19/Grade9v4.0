Build the idea

Specify the body and actual displacement before applying the constant-force formula. Only the force component along displacement contributes: W = F·Δr = F Δr cos θ. The angle is between the force and the displacement, not between force and a page axis chosen arbitrarily.

Three equal-magnitude forces act separately during the same rightward displacement. Predict each work sign and justify why the perpendicular case is zero even for a large force.
