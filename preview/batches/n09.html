WPE-N09 · Friction work belongs to a chosen body and frame

Synthetic source-to-Core1A candidate · QRT join absent · Blueprint draft · Topic adapter not approved · Canonical contact-work scientific warrant missing and unqualified.

What must be specified?

First choose the body, inertial frame and type of contact. For a block sliding on a fixed horizontal surface, kinetic friction opposes sliding and therefore points opposite the block's displacement, giving negative work on the block. That conclusion is not a universal friction-work sign law for other bodies or moving surfaces.

Fresh protected attempt

A 3 kg block slides 4 m right on a fixed horizontal surface. The coefficient of kinetic friction is 0.2 and g=10 m/s². No other vertical forces act. Find work by kinetic friction, and explain why the formula cannot be copied to a moving conveyor belt without re-examining the contact.

Hint 1 · ClarifyOn a fixed horizontal plane with no vertical acceleration and no other vertical force, N=mg.
Hint 2 · ConnectMagnitude fk=μk N, but the work sign comes from friction's direction relative to this block's displacement.
Worked reconstruction · reveal after attemptN=30 N, fk=6 N left, Wf=−6×4=−24 J. On a moving belt, friction may point along a chosen body's motion in the ground frame while still opposing relative slip at contact. You need the belt velocity, contact kinematics and chosen body's displacement to decide its work sign.
Diagnose and transferWrong route: 'friction work is always negative' or 'static friction always does zero work.' Repair: fix the body and frame, determine friction from relative contact slip (or static-contact constraints), then compute the work of the force on the selected body. Rolling contacts and deforming materials require different reviewed models.
