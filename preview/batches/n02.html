Why the constant-force shortcut fails

A force that changes with position cannot generally be replaced by its final value. Split motion into small displacements: each gives approximately F(x)Δx. Add their signed contributions, then use the integral when the required continuity/integrability conditions hold.

Above the x-axis contributes positive work; below contributes negative work. Geometric area without a sign is not the work ledger.
