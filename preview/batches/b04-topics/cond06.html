ILL-06 · Radial versus tangential power

Synthetic practice: A 2 kg particle travels on a fixed-radius circle with speed v(t)=2t m/s for t≥0. At t=2 s, find radial power and the net instantaneous power.

Hint 1 · DirectionRadial force is perpendicular to the particle's instantaneous velocity.
Hint 2 · Speed changeaₜ=dv/dt=2 m/s². Speed at t=2 s is 4 m/s.
Worked result (separate reveal)P_radial=0 W. P_net=maₜv=2×2×4=16 W. This agrees with d(½mv²)/dt.
