ILL-11 · Chain energy and initial kinetic state

Synthetic practice: An ideal uniform chain of length 6 m and mass 3 kg begins at rest, with 2 m hanging over a smooth table. When all 6 m hang, find the kinetic energy. g=10 m/s².

Hint 1 · Distributed massFor a uniform chain, U(h)=−mg h²/(2L), using the tabletop as zero.
Hint 2 · Energy balanceEvaluate U for h=2 and h=6. Include initial kinetic energy in Kf+Uf=Ki+Ui.
Worked result (separate reveal)Ui=−10 J, Uf=−90 J. If Ki=0, Kf=80 J. If Ki=5 J, Kf=85 J.
