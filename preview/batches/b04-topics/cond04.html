ILL-04 · Friction on a fixed incline

Synthetic practice: A 2 kg block travels 5 m down a fixed incline. sin θ = 0.6, cos θ = 0.8, μₖ = 0.25, g = 10 m/s². Find gravity, kinetic-friction and normal work.

Hint 1 · Normal forceThe plane is fixed; the normal reaction equals mg cos θ in this model.
Hint 2 · Work signsGravity has a downhill component; sliding friction acts uphill; normal is perpendicular to travel.
Worked route (request separately)N = 16 N. Wg = mg(5)sin θ = +60 J; Wf = −μₖN(5) = −20 J; WN = 0 J. Net work = +40 J.
