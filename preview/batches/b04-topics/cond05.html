ILL-05 · Gravitational power with nonzero entry speed

Synthetic practice: A 2 kg block enters a smooth 30° incline with speed 3 m/s, then travels 4 m down it. Take g = 10 m/s². Find the bottom speed and instantaneous power of gravity.

Hint 1 · Retain entry speedUse v² = u² + 2g s sin θ, not a formula assuming release from rest.
Hint 2 · Identify whose powerPower delivered by gravity is mgv sin θ along this plane.
Worked result (separate reveal)v² = 9 + 40 = 49, so v = 7 m/s. Pgravity = 2×10×7×0.5 = 70 W.
