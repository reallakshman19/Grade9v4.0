ILL-12 · Spring contact versus detachment

Synthetic practice: A 2 kg block presses against, but is not attached to, a light spring (k=200 N/m; natural length 0.8 m). It starts at rest with the spring compressed to 0.6 m on a frictionless table. Find its speed at wall distances 0.7 m, 0.8 m and 1.0 m.

Hint 1 · Elastic energyInitial stored energy is ½k(0.2)²=4 J.
Hint 2 · Contact boundaryWhile in contact use U(x)=½k(0.8−x)². Once contact ends at 0.8 m, the unattached block travels freely.
Worked result (separate reveal)At 0.7 m, U=1 J, K=3 J, v=√3 m/s. At 0.8 m, v=2 m/s. At 1.0 m, v remains 2 m/s.

Unattached block, light Hookean spring, initial rest and negligible friction. Native Conditions finding OPEN; academic admissions 0.
