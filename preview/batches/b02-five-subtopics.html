01 · Vector displacement is final minus initial

An orientation-and-component diagram to support the decisive move before the dot product.

Try this new problem

A particle moves from A = (1, 2) m to B = (5, −1) m under a constant force F = (2, 3) N. Determine the displacement vector and the signed work. Explain why a position vector alone is not the displacement.

Hint

Subtract A from B component by component. Then project the constant force onto that displacement using the dot product.

Synthetic worked route · not an approved key

Δr = B − A = (4, −3) m. Work = F · Δr = (2)(4) + (3)(−3) = −1 J. The negative sign reflects the net opposition of the force projection to the displacement. Position alone does not encode the motion interval.

Review obligation: Check mathematical correspondence of arrows, coordinate conventions and point-of-application assumptions; official Illustration 1 stem remains separate. Native asset attachment and science review are OPEN.

02 · Force ledger on a rising block

Separate tension, weight and displacement before summing work.

Try this new problem

A 3 kg block accelerates upward at 1 m/s² over 2 m; use g = 10 m/s². Determine tension, work by tension, work by gravity and net work on the block.

Hint

For the chosen block, first use T − mg = ma. Work uses the direction of each individual force against the same upward displacement.

Synthetic worked route · not an approved key

T = m(g + a) = 3(10 + 1) = 33 N. Work by tension = +33 × 2 = +66 J; work by gravity = −30 × 2 = −60 J. Net work = +6 J, consistent with ma × s = 3 × 1 × 2.

Review obligation: Force arrows must represent forces on the chosen body, not motion arrows; independent Physics check and native binding remain OPEN.

03 · Work as accumulated area under F(x)

The graph makes the varying force visible before the integral is evaluated.

Try this new problem

A force acts along the x direction with F(x) = (4 + 2x) N, where x is measured in metres. Determine the work from x = 0 to x = 3 m by both geometry and integration.

Hint

Split the region into a rectangle of height 4 and a triangular increase of height 6 over width 3. Or evaluate ∫₀³(4 + 2x) dx.

Synthetic worked route · not an approved key

Rectangle: 4 × 3 = 12 J. Triangle: ½ × 3 × 6 = 9 J. Total = 21 J. Integral: [4x + x²]₀³ = 12 + 9 = 21 J. Both routes count the same area.

Review obligation: Ensure graph domain, dimensional meanings and calculus prerequisites are appropriate to the learner. Not a source-verified reproduction; native binding OPEN.

04 · Incline power depends on the starting speed

Do not import release-from-rest into an underspecified source question.

Try this clarified synthetic version

A 2 kg block is released from rest on a smooth fixed incline of length 4 m, angle 30°, with g = 10 m/s². Determine gravitational power at the bottom. What changes if initial speed is not given?

Hint

For the clarified example, use v² = 0² + 2gL sinθ. The instantaneous rate supplied by gravity is P = (mg sinθ)v.

Synthetic worked route · not an approved key

Down-slope acceleration is 10 sin30° = 5 m/s². Hence v² = 2 × 5 × 4 = 40 and v = √40 m/s. Gravity's tangent component is 2 × 10 × 0.5 = 10 N. P = 10√40 ≈ 63.25 W. If u is unspecified, the general expression is P = mg sinθ √(u² + 2gL sinθ); no unique numerical value follows.

05 · Radial force versus tangential power

A perpendicular radial force changes direction but contributes zero instantaneous power.

Try this new problem

A 2 kg particle follows a circle of fixed radius 3 m and speeds up with v(t) = 2t m/s for t ≥ 0. Find the net instantaneous power at t = 2 s. Explain the radial contribution.

Hint

P = F · v. The inward force is perpendicular to velocity. The tangent acceleration equals dv/dt.

Synthetic worked route · not an approved key

At t = 2 s, speed v = 4 m/s and tangential acceleration dv/dt = 2 m/s². Tangential force is 2 × 2 = 4 N. Net power is 4 × 4 = 16 W. Radial force does zero instantaneous work rate because it is perpendicular to velocity, though it changes the direction of motion.

Review obligation: Confirm the vector decomposition, constant-radius assumption and no implied zero net power; native asset/cognitive calibration and source qualification remain OPEN.
