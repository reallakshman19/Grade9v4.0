Try: A 1 kg block enters an incline at 5 m/s and rises 0.5 m. Over that interval an applied force does +2 J of work and kinetic friction does −4 J. Take g=10 m/s². Find final kinetic energy and speed.

Hint 1 · The systemTake the block + Earth as the system; then ΔU=mgΔh=+5 J.
Hint 2 · All transfersConserve total energy accounting for push and friction: Kf+Uf=Ki+Ui+2 J−4 J.
Worked calculationKi=½(1)(5²)=12.5 J, ΔU=5 J, external-plus-friction work=−2 J. So Kf=12.5−5−2=5.5 J. vf=√(2Kf/m)=√11≈3.32 m/s. Omitting a work contribution changes the result.

Boundary: inertial frame, stated body–Earth system and force-work contributions along one fixed travel interval. This does not establish a universal friction sign law for other bodies/frames.
