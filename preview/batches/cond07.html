WEP07-03 · Potential-energy conditions

Independent synthetic illustration, not the original NSEP question. Native R3 Conditions finding OPEN. Zero academic admissions.

Try: A particle of mass 2 kg has U(x)=[4(1 m/x)² − 4(1 m/x)] J for x>0. It starts at rest at x=1 m. Find its conserved energy, accessible positive-x region, and its maximum speed.

Hint 1 — accessible motionAt the initial point E=U(1)=0. Allowed positions satisfy K=E−U≥0.
Hint 2 — maximum speedDifferentiate U or complete the square in 1/x; speed is greatest at the minimum accessible U.
Worked resultFor x≥1 m, U≤0 and K=−U. U reaches −1 J at x=2 m, giving Kmax=1 J and vmax=√(2K/m)=1 m/s. Speed tends to zero only as x→∞, not at another finite turning point.
