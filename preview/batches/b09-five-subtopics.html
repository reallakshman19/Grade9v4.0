Fresh independent-of-exam practice

Synthetic physics check: a 2 kg particle starts from rest under ideal constant NET mechanical power P=9 W on a horizontal line. Derive s(t), its shape, and s and v at t=4 s.

Attempt not yet confirmed; hints and worked route are locked in this preview.

Hint 1: For this ideal model, P=dK/dt and K(0)=0; integrate energy first.
Hint 2: ½mv²=Pt; v(t)=√(2P/m)·t¹ᐟ². Integrate ds/dt=v.
Worked reconstruction: v(t)=3√t m/s and s(t)=2t³ᐟ² m. Thus s(4)=16 m, v(4)=6 m. The curve starts with zero slope and is increasing/concave up for t>0.

Model conditions

Synthetic task, not the exam: a 3 kg block enters an ideal 100 N/m spring at 2 m/s on a horizontal surface. A known constant kinetic friction force of 5 N acts throughout compression. Find maximum compression.

Attempt not yet confirmed; hints and worked route are locked in this preview.

Hint 1: At the first turning instant the speed is zero; initial K=½mv².
Hint 2: Account for the dissipated work 5x and spring storage ½kx² over the SAME compression distance x.
Worked reconstruction: Initial K=6 J. Set 6=5x+50x². Rearrange 50x²+5x−6=0, with positive root x=(−5+√1225)/100=0.30 m. Spring stores 4.5 J; friction consumes 1.5 J.

Model conditions

Fresh independent-of-exam practice

Synthetic contrast: a 1 kg bob on a massless 2 m string in gravity g=10 m/s². Compare (A) a CONSTANT horizontal force that drives it from rest at the bottom to a first momentary stop at θ=60°, with (B) the force required to hold it in STATIC equilibrium at θ=60°. Ignore losses.

Attempt not yet confirmed; hints and worked route are locked in this preview.

Hint 1: For (A) the horizontal work F·Δx equals mgΔh at turning. For (B), balance tangential forces at the final fixed angle.
Hint 2: Δx=Lsin60°, Δh=L(1−cos60°). Constant Fturn=mg(1−cos60°)/sin60°. For equilibrium Feq=mg tan60°.
Worked reconstruction: Constant-force turning: Fturn=10/√3 N≈5.77 N. Static equilibrium at 60°: Feq=10√3 N≈17.32 N. The different answers reflect different dynamical constraints; neither automatically resolves the original exam wording.

Model conditions

Massless taut string, vertical plane, horizontal force on bob, frictionless pivot. Case A initial rest and momentary turning; Case B balanced forces and static end-state. Actual exam wording/key and model selection remain unverified.
