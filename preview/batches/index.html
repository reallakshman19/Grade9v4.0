How this library grows

Each batch is a bounded five-subtopic deliverable with a distinct page, source mapping, test evidence and unresolved-review notes. New pages append to this index without replacing existing batch HTML.

  1. Prepare exactly five new synthetic examples and keep them isolated from protected exam stems and figures.
  2. Verify the output and identify academic versus technical gates separately.
  3. Publish the new page at a permanent URL under this directory, then add its entry to the batch manifest.
  4. Confirm that the site serves the new page and preserves the older links.
