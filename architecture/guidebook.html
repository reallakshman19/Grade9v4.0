00 · The whole method on one page

Navigation often reads “subtopic → demands → QRT → Core1A.” That is a useful learner-content planning view, but not the full academic dependency chain. The subject’s authority, Blueprint and topic adapter must be consulted before task classification; and source exposition reaches Core1A through a distinct route.

SUBJECT: Physics / Mathematics / Chemistry
     ↓ current Shared rules + subject Blueprint + topic adapter
CHAPTER → SUBTOPIC / prerequisite construction
     ├─ QUESTION ROUTE: exam + textbook questions

     │    → qualified native Core2 question
     │

          → Core1B fresh protected reconstruction

02 · From chapter to subtopic—without losing the task

A subtopic describes the conceptual construction to teach (for example, signed work from force and displacement). A question/task is an exact source item that may demand that construction, perhaps with additional conditions. A representation is an explanatory or problem-specific diagram. These are related, not interchangeable IDs.

  1. Choose the subject and chapter from an authorized scope. Physics → WPE exists; no first Mathematics or Chemistry chapter is approved.
  2. Give each construction a bounded competency: what learner-owned transformation or judgment should be possible later?
  3. Record prerequisites and the directional distinction (e.g. from potential to force is not proof of the reverse from force to potential).
  4. Identify exact source tasks only through their individual identities; do not assign a blanket D-band to a whole subtopic.
  5. Keep authored practice examples separate from authenticated direct-exam evidence.
Output: a subject/chapter/subtopic construction statement, prerequisites, source role and explicit unresolved dependencies—not yet an approved lesson or QRT cell.

03 · Source-based requirements and exact custody

Every claim starts with an identified occurrence, immutable or versioned source bytes where available, locator and declared provenance. Scientific correctness, official examination identity and answer-key custody are three different judgments.

Exam questionPaper, session, exact task/variant, question text, options, figures, answer-key source and separately verified identityCoaching solution ≠ authenticated official key; an image placeholder ≠ original figure
Textbook questionBook/edition/page/problem number, exact stem, figures, rights, source classificationA book problem ≠ past examination evidence
Exposition / syllabusExact passage/page/figure and scope of each candidate claimSource wording ≠ fully qualified general law

Protocol: collect → fingerprint → resolve duplicate versus variant → bind exact figure/key independently → document rights and source role → fail closed. Where original graphs are missing, create a recovery task; do not substitute a newly drawn diagram and claim the original was authenticated.

04 · Two evidence routes meet at a construction requirement

ROUTE A · QUESTIONS

What must the learner do?

Exam/textbook question → source identity → SL1 → SL2 proposed decisive operation and demand → independent QRT → exact question/requirement role.

ROUTE B · EXPOSITION

What must be taught to make that possible?

Book/syllabus/figure → exact units → candidate claims + conditions → warranted law → construction obligations → appropriate Core1A instruction.

The join is not simply a matching keyword such as “spring” or “energy.” It needs a reviewed connection between the exact task’s decisive transformation and the specific passage, law, conditions and teaching evidence. A construction can be prepared without a reviewed QRT task, but it cannot claim to qualify that task until the join is adjudicated.

05 · Native SL1 → optional P1 → proposed SL2

SL1 — identity and eligibility

SL1 records exact source, answer, paper, figure, integrity, version and practice fitness gates. It may show that two bytes or records match; that is not independent scientific truth or official authentication.

P1 — only when an actual learner’s initial evidence exists

Optional P1 can preserve the real first response before any hint/key exposure, including sequence history. If missing, label it absent/unknown. Missing P1 must not block static question analysis or create a false claim of independent mastery.

SL2 — requirement proposal, not a verdict

SL2 describes: primary intellectual operation candidate; alternative credible operations; shortest valid route; decisive reasoning move; model validity; five intrinsic-demand factors; and actual evidence links. A long explanation, many hints or the label “D3” does not certify intrinsic task difficulty.

Deliverables: version-pinned SL1 record and checker output, optional provenance of P1, and PROPOSED / UNREVIEWED SL2 evidence. The independent decision remains outside SL2. No new “SL3” is introduced by this workflow.

Basis: SL skills · R2 gate mapping.

06 · Derive demand from the exact shortest task route

Current reviewed Physics SL2 proposals use five intrinsic-demand factors, each scored in the repository candidate examples on a 0–2 scale. The evidence, not the arithmetic alone, determines the claim. The displayed factor names below match the recorded rubric keys.

FactorWhat to inspect in the exact taskCheck against inflation
concept_model_selectionIs a law/model obvious, chosen among rivals or requiring justification?Do not reward a verbose explanation of a routine law.
representation_translationWhat graph, diagram, coordinate or source model must be interpreted?Missing picture is a source defect, not intrinsic difficulty.
reasoning_chain_lengthHow many necessary nonroutine transitions must a shortest valid route take?Count decisions, not the number of written steps.

Do not invent D-band score thresholds here. The subject’s approved rubric, exact factor evidence and independent task decision must supply any final D-band. D1/D2 common Blueprint remains SPEC_PENDING; a reviewed D2 support question cannot be force-fitted into D3/D4 cells.

07 · Independent QRT: seven operations, one exact task verdict

The current common operation vocabulary is RETRIEVE, EXPLAIN, APPLY, MODEL, REPRESENT, SYNTHESIZE and JUSTIFY. This list is not a universal Physics-to-Chemistry transfer of scoring or examples.

For each exact task, QRT must independently establish the decisive primary operation, credible alternatives, shortest valid route, five-factor demand and coverage role. The three recorded roles are DIRECT_EXAM_EVIDENCE, PREREQUISITE_BRIDGE and AUTHORED_TRANSFER. Only authenticated direct-exam evidence can qualify the respective direct-exam target; a bridge or authored example is a different contribution.

Source-only first-pass protocol

  1. Authorize and scope a reviewer, exact task/version, subject rubric, figure inputs and seal destination.
  2. Withhold prior labels, the author’s worked answer, answer key and academic reconciliation until the independent pass is sealed.
  3. Reviewer independently solves and records the decisive route, model conditions, operation, demand evidence, ambiguity and source limitations.
  4. Seal the original first-pass report and digest; only afterwards compare with prior opinions and keys.
  5. Adjudicate source role and academic admission separately; no answer exposure is retroactively “blind.”
Anyone who has read this guide’s named prior task outcomes is author-exposed. They should not claim to perform a blind independent first pass on those same tasks. Give a new reviewer the source-only reviewer entry, not this guide or the cumulative reader.

The questions below can guide an author or reviewer without treating either party’s first guess as a verdict.

10 · Source passage → conditional warrant → teaching obligation

An explanation is insufficient if it hides the conditions under which the law is valid. Build a claim ledger with exact source passage, proposed meaning, assumptions, exceptions, subject expert status and the direction required by the assessed task.

11 · Core2 — exact question construction

Core2’s question route consumes authorized exact source task records with distinct paper/key/figure custody. It must preserve answer type (including multiple-correct), source identity, the correct scientific model, learner attempt and staged hints without leaking the answer through accessibility text, thumbnails or keyboard state.

  1. Check that source, key and figure bytes have the required provenance and rights.
  2. Bind actual question IDs and source roles to native bank/manifest/template; do not copy a public synthetic exercise and relabel it as an exam original.
  3. Represent conditions that are actually in the task; omitted initial data is not silently repaired by changing the source.
  4. Render native output and audit keyboard, state restoration, reveal order, duplicate identifiers, answer semantics and every displayed figure.
  5. Retain independently reviewed scientific/academic and Owner gates. A successful render is a technical receipt only.

For this Owner-scoped WPE route, completed and accepted Core2 scope precedes new native Core1A authoring; preparation dossiers can exist in parallel. No step of this guide grants admission to the draft reader.

12 · Core1A teaching and Core1B fresh reconstruction: B1–B7

Core1A must actually teach the target reasoning; Core1B must elicit a genuinely fresh, protected reconstruction rather than the same worked example copied back. The construction binder needs evidence of every obligation below, independent of whether a page looks complete.

ObligationEvidence question

B2 — Construction signatureWhat distinctive reasoning move, prerequisites and model selection define it?

14 · Worked method illustration: Physics → WPE → signed work

This is a newly authored example of how to plan and document a construction. It is not an authenticated examination question, approved Blueprint placement or QRT score.

  1. Subtopic and direction. Signed work for a constant force over a specified displacement; future learner must evaluate the dot product and interpret the sign.
  2. Fresh synthetic check. A particle moves by Δr=(3, −2) m under constant F=(4, −1) N. The intended check is W=4·3+(−1)(−2)=14 J. State inertial frame, force constant over the displacement and one particle.
  3. SL1/source role. AUTHORED_TRANSFER; original exam-paper custody and official key are not claimed. Record the origin and version of this newly written task.
  4. SL2 proposal. Describe the decisive move (final-minus-initial displacement, componentwise signed projection) and plausible misconception (multiply magnitudes; lose the negative sign). Score no D-band unless an independently reviewed task/rubric actually supports it.
  5. QRT HOLD. No examiner has independently adjudicated this authored task; it cannot be counted toward the 21 original D3/D4 exam targets.
  6. Blueprint/adapter HOLD. No approved WPE adapter selected; the draft D3/D4 Physics matrix is not a qualification. A lesson designer may use it only as candidate design guidance.
  7. Source/warrant. Bind the constant-force dot-product law with its conditions and exact teaching direction; request independent Physics verification for academic use.
  8. Core1A candidate. Teach projection, two contrasting signs, why perpendicular force gives zero work and an actual incorrect-route diagnosis.
  9. Core1B fresh candidate. Use different force/displacement vectors and change one sign; protect the first attempt before hints; verify transfer without copying the example.
  10. Disposition. Technical illustration may render publicly as synthetic; academic and native authoring status stays HOLD.

The example deliberately separates a mathematically correct result from the nontrivial evidence needed to say the lesson is qualified.

Structural basis: Construction-to-task distinctions · SL1/SL2 candidate roles. Numerical example above is new explanatory material, not sourced from the repository.

17 · The author's decision worksheet: make the reasoning inspectable

This is a practical reasoning record, not a hidden transcript or an academic verdict. Before a new agent creates a lesson, it should be able to explain the exact source claim, the competing physical models, the assumptions that choose between them, and what a child must work out independently. Record these decisions in ordinary, reproducible language so a subject expert can audit them.

18 · How to use QRT without guessing a score or contaminating review

QRT classifies the intellectual demand of an exact task; it does not author the Physics solution. The author may inspect source/SL1 and write a clearly labelled SL2 proposal. An independent QRT first pass requires a separately appointed reviewer who has not read this guidebook, author solutions, coverage labels or prior verdicts for the source. Dispatch the approved source-only protocol and seal its evidence before exposing author outcomes. Anyone using this author-exposed guide must not claim a blind first-pass result for the same source.

  1. Define the decisive move: Reconstruct the shortest physically valid answer route; isolate the act without which the task cannot be solved. “Contains a graph” does not automatically mean REPRESENT. “Uses an integral” does not automatically mean D4.
  2. Compare all seven operations: RETRIEVE (defining fact); EXPLAIN (physical cause); APPLY (known method correctly mapped); MODEL (select system, law and assumptions); REPRESENT (translate representations preserving meaning); SYNTHESIZE (connect dependent operations); JUSTIFY (derive/defend claim with exception check). Record the primary candidate and why a rival is secondary.
  3. Evaluate demand using five factors: model selection, representation translation, reasoning chain, computational load and exception sensitivity. Only an authorized QRT review supplies the five actual 0–2 scores and sum. The reference mapping is D1 = 0–2; D2 = 3–5; D3 = 6–7; D4 = 8–10. An author proposing “D3” must not fill in invented reviewer scores.
  4. Keep task types apart: original exam, textbook source, authored guided anchor, MAIN and instance are different evidence roles. Each may have a different dominant operation and demand. The D2 practice instance supporting a D4 MAIN is not a D4 original-exam question.
  5. Report what actually exists: AUTHOR_PROPOSAL, FIRST_PASS_RECORDED, NOT_RUN, HOLD or a real independently reviewed receipt as appropriate; never turn a derived lesson link or a browser PASS into QRT acceptance.

19 · Blueprint + topic adapter: an exact binding procedure

The Physics Blueprint specifies common cognitive structure; the WPE adapter supplies topic-specific laws, variables, diagrams, misconceptions and scientific limits. The current Physics D3/D4 Blueprint v0.2.0 is DRAFT_OWNER_REVIEW, D3.1 is paused and no single WPE topic adapter is Owner-selected. Those statuses cannot be upgraded because a lesson renders successfully.

  1. Pin bytes, not just labels. In the actual branch record the Blueprint path and SHA-256, blueprint_id, version, status, one adapter path/version/digest and its actual Owner selection receipt. If no selection exists, say UNSELECTED. Do not pick among equal-version but different-content WPE candidates yourself.
  2. Check QRT evidence. A reviewed D3/D4 operation may propose a candidate cell; an author-only SL2 proposal gives a provisional mapping. D1/D2 tasks have no cell in this D3/D4-only Blueprint; record D1_D2_BLUEPRINT_SPEC_PENDING, rather than borrowing D3.
  3. Find the cell mechanically. The seven-column order is RETRIEVE (1), EXPLAIN (2), APPLY (3), MODEL (4), REPRESENT (5), SYNTHESIZE (6), JUSTIFY (7). Thus MODEL/D3 suggests D3.4; JUSTIFY/D4 suggests D4.7. Validate levels[D3|D4], columns[1..7] and cells[D3.4] together, including its template reference, reveal rules, MAIN/instance obligations, diagram stages and hints. A string such as PHY-WPE-D3.4 is a candidate identity, not approval.
  4. Bind the disciplinary law to each step. The adapter should say whether the source is a string or a captive rail, whether the wall is inside or outside, which force does work, the coordinate sign, what can detach, and whether the source figure is authenticated. Common Blueprint fields should not be rewritten to encode WPE-specific exceptions.
  5. Produce a gap note instead of improvising. When required adapter/body/diagram/hint/instance evidence is missing, keep the cell unselected and record the missing rule with a bounded proposal. Do not quietly promote an author-built lab into a qualified native tile.

Plausible wrong pathA real tempting alternative, the first invalid step and a counterexample that rejects itDoes the explanation teach model selection, not just correct arithmetic?
Close the ideaIndependent exit check and a changed assumption that limits the modelCould the child explain both the valid rule and when not to use it?

Design the visualization to test a statement. A slider must change a meaningful physical quantity and its matching arrows/units. Make diagrams scene-matched: the initial state, the contact-loss instant and the post-release trajectory are not interchangeable pictures. An attractive but geometrically inaccurate drawing can teach the wrong law. Author-produced figures must never be passed off as verified original examination figures.

Concrete practice — C6H outside a smooth dome. Draw gravity downward and the exterior normal outward; while touching, use mg cosθ − N = mv²/R with N ≥ 0. Combine this with the energy ledger. At the first N=0, stop drawing a constrained circle and change to free flight. The familiar h=2R/3 from crown rest is a vanishing-perturbation limit: exact symmetric rest has zero tangential force and cannot spontaneously begin descending. Compare with a captive groove or a nonzero crown launch. This is authored, conditional teaching—not independent verification of EX-N31's original figure or key.

Additional stop checks: At a spring-tether's exact opposite point, initial spring force can be purely radial, making exact rest a stationary unstable equilibrium; a positive energy difference alone does not initiate motion. With an ideal string, a negative required tension means the assumed taut path is impossible. For a rough tube, the total contact work may be obtained from endpoints, but local friction distribution is not determined by loop count.

21 · Core1B construction studio: protect a new inference, not a new number

Write the fresh question before adding hints. Define Y (the taught principle), Z (the bridge the learner may use) and W (the decisive inference that must belong to the learner). A fresh Task B should change the physical setting or governing condition; merely replacing 2 kg with 3 kg leaves W unchanged.

Worked example: string vs captive groove

Core1A teaches Y and Z

Y: energy sets hypothetical speed along a path. Z: radial force balance tests whether the assumed constraint is physically feasible. For a light taut string at top, T + mg = mv²/R and T ≥ 0; a two-sided captive groove can exert radial force in either direction.

Learner decides W

Given a new moving system, which constraint applies and which reaction-force sign is allowed? The learner must defend this before a complete worked top-point condition is revealed. A student can calculate a positive speed and still select an impossible string model.

22 · Lessons learned from real WPE rounds: test the assumption the formula hides

Recent R3/R4 authoring produced valid conditional calculations, but also exposed ways an otherwise green test suite can miss a scientific defect. Use these as negative controls, not as a license to promote any original question.

Operational lesson: A unit test that reproduces the authored numerical answer is weak if it uses the same wrong initial assumption. Every scientific construction needs an independent derivation, a physically impossible or competing-model negative case, a valid limiting case and a test that would fail if the wrong law were used.
