12 ordered actions an agent can follow

Do not skip a step to make a question reach D3 or D4. At every HOLD, record the reason and continue to the next question; never invent missing evidence.

  1. Choose role and pin sourcesRecord the commit SHA, question ID, source path and available receipts. Default to NOT_ADMITTED.
  2. Verify exact source identityInspect original stem/figures/options/conditions and official key separately. Missing original custody = HOLD.
  3. Record SL1/SL2 as proposalsSL1 is identity/custody; SL2 proposes the decisive move and five difficulty factors. Optional P1 is not mandatory.
  4. Dispatch independent QRT when authorizedSource-only; withhold prior answer, suggested band, solutions and labels; require actual sealed first-pass bytes.
  5. Compare the five-factor scoreSum five 0–2 scores with evidence. D1 0–2, D2 3–5, D3 6–7, D4 8–10.
  6. Choose exactly one decisive operationRetrieve, Explain, Apply, Model, Represent, Synthesize, Justify. Use actual Blueprint operation boundaries.
  7. Route to a candidate Blueprint cellOnly D3/D4 → D3/D4.1–.7. Reviewed D1/D2 is never promoted. Unreviewed proposed labels yield candidate-only routing.
  8. Bind one approved topic adapterWPE facts/diagrams/conditions belong in adapter; Blueprint owns generic cognitive rules. No selected exact hash = HOLD.
  9. Build full Core1ATeach conventions, prerequisites, justified steps, different worked anchor, wrong route and independent exit check.
  10. Build protected Core1BOne band-carrying MAIN plus per-instance six-stage cycle; save first attempt, then reconstruct, diagnose, repair and boundary-test.
  11. Check B1–B7 and native evidenceVerify target, signature, sufficiency, transfer, attempt protection, diagnosis, Physics/provenance and exact version pins.
  12. Decide honestly and recheck on changeAcademic admission requires real independent decisions and Owner receipts. Student practice may remain usable under a clearly marked HOLD.

QRT: ask what the task requires, not what subject it mentions

ColumnDecisive learner actionObservable evidence
1 · RetrieveChoose the fact/category by defining conditionVerdict + deciding condition
2 · ExplainEstablish a causal mechanismEvery link warranted, changed-condition prediction
3 · ApplyExecute a known relation validlyApplicability, signs, substitutions and check
4 · ModelChoose law/system/assumptionsDefensible choice plus applicability conditions
5 · RepresentTranslate graph/diagram/words/equationInvariant and consistent back-translation
6 · SynthesizeCoordinate dependent conceptsBridge quantity, order and second-route/invariant check
7 · JustifyEstablish a physical conclusionWarrant, assumptions and boundary case

Five scored factors: concept/model selection; representation translation; reasoning chain length; algebra/computational load; trap/exception sensitivity. Each must have an integer 0–2 and a task-specific evidence statement. D3 = 6–7; D4 = 8–10. Neither a nice lesson nor an SL2 D3 label is enough.

Try it with a genuine reviewer, not an invented one. The [first-pass schema] below separates source-only packet from reviewer result and a sealed receipt. A source-only packet with unbound original/figure remains NOT READY. Structural validation cannot establish reviewer independence.
